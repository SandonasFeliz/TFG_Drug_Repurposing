Design and development of a platform for the comparison and explanation of drug repurposing methods using biomedical networks and artificial intelligence

Drug repurposing looks for new therapeutic uses of known medicines. This platform runs several methods on a biomedical graph and shows a ranking of candidate drugs for the disease you choose, together with the evidence behind each result.

Results are computational hypotheses to support research, not clinical recommendations.
